Privacy Policy – Spin Throne Slots

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Spin Throne Slots ("the game") is developed by Spin Throne Games.

Your game progress

Playing the game needs no account, and it does not ask for your real name, email or other personal details (only registered playtesters can choose to sign in: see Playtest program; at the live roulette table you can choose a table name: see Live roulette table; the same name shows on the Daily Sprint leaderboard). Your game progress (virtual coins, level, settings, achievements and messages in your in-game mail) is saved on your device. Uninstalling the game, or using "Reset progress" in Settings, deletes it there.

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game also keeps a copy of your progress in your Google account (Google Play Games saved games, stored by Google in a hidden area of your Google Drive that only this game can use), so you can get it back on a new phone. We never receive that copy. Settings > Account shows whether it is saved. You can delete it from your Google account at any time (for example in Google Drive's settings under "Manage apps", or in your Google Play Games settings).

Shared jackpots

Some machines have jackpots shared by all players. While you are online, the game sends our jackpot server (run for us by Cloudflare) a random ID that the game creates on your device, how many spins you played on those machines, and the jackpots you won there. The random ID is not linked to your name, your device, your advertising ID or anything else about you. The server uses this only to run the shared jackpots and to stop cheating (daily limits per ID). It deletes the ID with your counts after at most two days and with your jackpot wins after at most a week; it tells other players that "a player" won, never who. Cloudflare sees your IP address to deliver the data, but the server does not store it (see the Cloudflare Privacy Policy). Offline, nothing is sent and you play for jackpots of your own.

To show news and gifts in the in-game mail, the game downloads the current messages from our game server (run for us by Cloudflare; older versions of the game download them from this website, hosted by GitHub). With the request the game sends only its Player ID (shown in Settings): a short code taken from the random ID described above, so that a message written for just you (for example coins back from support after you contacted us) reaches only your game. The server doesn't keep a record of which IDs asked. Like any website, the server sees your IP address to deliver the messages but does not store it (see the Cloudflare Privacy Policy and the GitHub Privacy Statement).

Live roulette table

At the live roulette table, everyone online plays the same rounds. While you are at that table, the game sends our game server (run for us by Cloudflare) the random ID described above, the table name you chose (or none), the profile picture you picked in the game, and the bets you place, as you place them. Other players at the table see your table name, your picture, your Player ID if they open your seat, your bets, your reactions (chosen from a fixed list) and your wins. Please don't use your real name as your table name. Names are checked against a word filter; other players can report a name, and we look at reports and can reset a name that breaks the rules. The server keeps your table name with your random ID so that it can do this; it deletes it when you haven't been to the table for 180 days (unless that ID was blocked from choosing names after breaking the rules), and reports after 90 days. It keeps your bets only for the round being played and the table's latest numbers (not who bet on them). Your coins stay on your device: the server never holds or moves them. You can hide another player (they are hidden only on your device). The private table sends nothing. Cloudflare sees your IP address to deliver the data, but the server does not store it.

Daily Sprint tournaments

The Daily Sprint is a free daily tournament: everyone plays the same machine with the same number of spins, and the best scores win the game's virtual coins (they have no real-world value; there is nothing to buy). When you open the Tournaments tab, the game signs up with our game server (run for us by Cloudflare) using the random ID described above; the server gives your game a secret key so that only your game can play for that ID (the server keeps only a scrambled copy of the key, and your game keeps the key with your progress, including in your Google cloud save if you use one). The server decides every tournament spin and keeps, for that ID: your entries, every tournament spin and its result, your score, your place, your prizes and whether you claimed them, and simple counts of what you did (for example that you opened the Tournaments tab or the rules). The leaderboard shows other players your table name (or "Player 1234" if you have none) with your score and place; never your ID. After a Sprint you may answer a few optional questions (ratings and, if you like, a short comment): the server keeps the answers with your random ID so each Sprint is answered once; please don't write personal details in the comment. The server deletes tournament spins after 45 days, the counts after 120 days and the answers after a year; your entries and prizes stay so your history and claimed prizes stay correct. Your coins stay on your device. Cloudflare sees your IP address to deliver the data; the server does not store it (it is only used for a moment, in memory, to limit how many new sign-ups one address can make).

Advertising

The game shows ads from Google AdMob (optional reward videos and a banner in the casino lobby). To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's advertising software collects and processes data from your device, including your IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), your device's advertising ID and app set ID, how you interact with ads and the app (for example app launches, taps and video views), and diagnostic information. This data is sent to Google encrypted. Learn how Google uses it at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Where the law requires (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and some US states), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice any time in Settings > Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings.

Purchases

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. The game never receives your payment details; it only learns whether you own "Remove Ads". Coins cannot be bought.

Children

The game is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Virtual coins

Coins in the game are virtual, have no real-world value, and cannot be purchased, redeemed or cashed out. The game does not offer real-money gambling.

Playtest program (registered testers only)

People who registered for our playtest can choose to sign in on the game's Playtest screen (Settings > Playtest) with the email address they registered with. Nobody else is affected: nothing in this section is collected unless you sign in and agree on that screen.

While you are signed in, the game sends our playtest database (run for us by Supabase) your email address and an account ID, when you play (measured by the server's clock), how long you actively play on slot machines (time without a touch for 90 seconds isn't counted), how many spins you complete, the game version and the kind of device (for example "android"), and any bug reports you write. It does not send your coins, wins, bets, contacts, location or anything else on your device. From the playtest sign-up form we keep only your email address, when you registered and who referred you (if you said); the rest of the form is not kept.

We use this only to run the playtest: to count your qualifying days, to review and send testers' rewards and referral rewards, and to fix bugs. Each tester sees only their own progress. You can sign out at any time to stop. "Delete my data" on the Playtest screen deletes your playtest account and data straight away. Without the game, email us from the address you registered with (subject "Delete my playtest account") and we delete them within 30 days. Records of rewards we have paid are kept as long as the law requires for our accounts. We delete all other playtest data within 90 days after the playtest ends. The data travels encrypted (HTTPS); Supabase keeps short-lived technical logs, including IP addresses, for security (see the Supabase Privacy Policy). This program is separate from Google Play's testing requirements.
