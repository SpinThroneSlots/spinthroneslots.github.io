Privacy Policy – Spin Throne Slots

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Spin Throne Slots ("the game") is developed by Spin Throne Games.

Your game progress

The game has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or other personal details. Your game progress (virtual coins, level, settings, achievements and messages in your in-game mail) is saved only on your device. Uninstalling the game, or using "Reset progress" in Settings, deletes it.

Shared jackpots

Some machines have jackpots shared by all players. While you are online, the game sends our jackpot server (run for us by Cloudflare) a random ID that the game creates on your device, how many spins you played on those machines, and the jackpots you won there. The random ID is not linked to your name, your device, your advertising ID or anything else about you. The server uses this only to run the shared jackpots and to stop cheating (daily limits per ID). It deletes the ID with your counts after at most two days and with your jackpot wins after at most a week; it tells other players that "a player" won, never who. Cloudflare sees your IP address to deliver the data, but the server does not store it (see the Cloudflare Privacy Policy). Offline, nothing is sent and you play for jackpots of your own.

To show news and gifts in the in-game mail, the game downloads the current messages from our game server (run for us by Cloudflare; older versions of the game download them from this website, hosted by GitHub). Nothing about you is sent with it; like any website, the server sees your IP address to deliver the messages but does not store it (see the Cloudflare Privacy Policy and the GitHub Privacy Statement).

Advertising

The game shows ads from Google AdMob (optional reward videos and a banner in the casino lobby). To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's advertising software collects and processes data from your device, including your IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), your device's advertising ID and app set ID, how you interact with ads and the app (for example app launches, taps and video views), and diagnostic information. This data is sent to Google encrypted. Learn how Google uses it at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Where the law requires (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and some US states), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice any time in Settings > Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings.

Purchases

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. The game never receives your payment details; it only learns whether you own "Remove Ads". Coins cannot be bought.

Children

The game is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Virtual coins

Coins in the game are virtual, have no real-world value, and cannot be purchased, redeemed or cashed out. The game does not offer real-money gambling.
